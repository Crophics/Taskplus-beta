Privacy

Taskplus (beta) is a small project run by one person, not a company. Last updated 18 November 2026.

The short version

  • Taskplus never sees or stores your Moodle password. It is used once to get an access token.
  • No ads, no analytics, no selling or sharing your data.
  • You can delete everything from Settings → Delete my data.

What is stored, and where

On your device: your assignments (including the ones imported from Moodle), your notes, settings and progress.

In your synced account (Google Firebase): your Google sign-in identifier, the assignments you added by hand, your courses and settings, and small records for imported assignments so they agree across your devices: what you finished, your notes, your own estimates, how long things took, and AI estimates.

On the Taskplus server (Cloudflare):

  • Your Moodle site address, user number, the courses you chose, and your Moodle access token, encrypted. This lets the server check Moodle for you while the app is closed.
  • A snapshot of your assignments (title, course, due date, status) so it can tell what is new or moved, and a short list of recent changes. Titles are kept up to about five months.
  • Your notification setup: a registration for each device you turned alerts on for (with a short device description and your time zone), and your alert choices.
  • Saved AI estimates, so asking again is free. They hold the estimate and its reason, not your assignment text.
  • Daily usage counts, which hold no content and expire in two days.

The AI estimates

AI estimates are off until you turn them on in Settings. Without them, Taskplus still gives a basic estimate using simple rules, and nothing about your assignments goes to an AI service. When AI estimates are on, Taskplus sends the assignment's title, course name, description, points, due time and submission type to the AI provider (Google Gemini or Anthropic Claude, whichever the server is set to use). If you turn on reading handouts, it also sends that assignment's PDF files. It also sends the title and minutes of up to 12 assignments you already finished, so the AI can judge similar work.

It does not send your notes, grades, account identifiers or login details.

Heads up: Taskplus currently uses Google's free Gemini tier. Under Google's terms for unpaid Gemini use, Google may use what is sent, and the answers it gives, to improve its products, and human reviewers may read it. Google advises against sending sensitive or personal information to unpaid services, which is why Taskplus sends only assignment details (never notes, grades, passwords or account identifiers). Because AI estimates are off by default, none of this happens unless you choose it; if you turn them on and later change your mind, switch them off again in Settings. If this changes (for example a paid Gemini plan, which Google treats differently), this page will say so.

Notifications

Alerts are delivered through Google's Firebase Cloud Messaging. Taskplus sends it a short message with the assignment name and due time so your phone can show it.

Other services involved

  • Cloudflare hosts the app and the server.
  • Google provides sign-in, sync, notification delivery and the Inter font, so Google sees your IP address when the page loads.
  • Your school's Moodle receives requests from the Taskplus server using your token, the same kind of requests the Moodle mobile app makes.

Deleting your data

In the app, open Settings → Delete my data. It removes your Moodle connection, snapshot, alerts, saved AI estimates and synced data from the server, signs you out, and clears the app's data on that device. Other devices keep their own copy until you delete there too. Your Moodle account is not affected. To stop Taskplus using Moodle without deleting everything, use Disconnect Moodle. You can also revoke the token from your Moodle security settings.

Questions or problems

Open an issue at github.com/Crophics/Taskplus-beta/issues. Please don't post passwords, tokens or personal details there.

This is a student project provided as is, with no guarantees. Check due dates in Moodle for anything that matters.
